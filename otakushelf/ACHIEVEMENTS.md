# Achievements

All 100 achievements (badges) that can be earned on the profile page. Badges are awarded automatically based on your stats.

## Rarity Guide

| Rarity | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| 🟢 Common | Easy to earn, happens naturally |
| 🔵 Uncommon | Requires some dedication |
| 🟣 Rare | Requires real commitment |
| 🟠 Epic | Very hard to reach |
| 🔴 Legendary | Almost nobody will get these |

## How Badges Are Awarded

- Badges are evaluated automatically from your list stats (counts, ratings, genres, account age, etc.).
- Earning a badge unlocks it permanently on your profile.
- Source of truth: `workers/src/utils/badgeDefinitions.js` (evaluated by `workers/src/services/badgeEngine.js`).
